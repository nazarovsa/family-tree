## Основной текст на английском языке

**Subject: Request for preservation of records, complete accounting and integrity review concerning inheritance distributions handled in Poland**

Dear Sir or Madam,

We are writing in connection with inheritance funds handled in Poland for the benefit of our late grandfather, **[FULL NAME]**, and members of his family approximately between **[YEAR]** and **[YEAR]**.

The inheritance originated from our grandfather's aunt, **[FULL NAME]**, who had emigrated to the United States before the Second World War. According to the information available to our family, a Red Cross entity was involved in the matter and delegated, appointed, recommended or otherwise referred its administration to a Polish law firm, believed to be **[NAME OF LAW FIRM]**.

The funds were distributed in two instalments. Our grandfather died in 2013, and the second instalment was administered or paid after his death.

Members of the family were residing in Russia. In order to receive payment, they were asked to sign forms on which the relevant monetary amounts had not been entered. They understood that signing the documents in that form was necessary in order to receive any part of the inheritance. The family was not provided with a complete accounting showing the gross amount received, the deductions made, the legal fees and expenses charged, the exchange rates applied, or the calculation of the amounts distributed.

These circumstances create a concern that the beneficiaries may not have received the full amounts due to them. At this stage, we are requesting an evidence-based review and are not asserting a final conclusion regarding the conduct of any individual or organisation.

We respectfully request that you:

1. Identify the Red Cross entity, department, office and case reference involved in this matter.
2. Identify the Polish law firm, individual lawyers and any other intermediaries appointed, recommended or engaged in connection with the inheritance.
3. Provide a copy of the mandate, referral, engagement agreement, power of attorney or other instrument under which the Polish firm acted.
4. Provide a complete accounting showing:
   - the gross amount of inheritance or other funds received;
   - the date, source, currency and amount of each incoming payment;
   - every tax, legal fee, commission, banking charge and other deduction;
   - the exchange rates applied;
   - the calculation of each beneficiary's share;
   - the date, currency and amount of every outgoing payment;
   - any amount retained, returned, transferred to another person or left undistributed.
5. Provide copies of all forms, authorisations, releases, powers of attorney and payment instructions allegedly signed by our grandfather or other members of the family, including copies showing the contents of those documents when they were processed.
6. Provide copies of bank confirmations, transfer records and other proof of payment, with information concerning unrelated persons redacted where necessary.
7. Explain how the second instalment was administered after our grandfather's death, who was treated as entitled to the funds, and which succession documents or legal authorities were relied upon.
8. Provide copies of relevant correspondence between the Red Cross entity and the Polish firm or other intermediaries.
9. Conduct an integrity review of the use of documents signed without stated monetary amounts and of any unexplained discrepancy between the funds received and the funds distributed.
10. Inform us whether the matter has previously been audited, reviewed, reported to an insurer, professional regulator or law-enforcement authority, and provide the reference number of any such proceeding where disclosure is permitted.

Please immediately preserve all paper and electronic records relating to this matter, including accounting records, bank records, correspondence, document versions, file notes, metadata, access logs and audit trails. Given the age of the matter and the possible application of limitation periods, please treat this letter as a formal notice to preserve evidence and suspend any routine destruction of relevant records.

Please confirm receipt of this request, assign a reference number and identify the person or department responsible for its review. If your organisation was not involved, please state this expressly and, if possible, identify the Red Cross entity or other organisation to which the matter was referred.

We are preparing documents establishing the family relationship, succession after our grandfather's death, the identities and authority of the applicants, and the payments received. A schedule of the affected beneficiaries and supporting documents will be supplied as a separate annex.

## Правила формулировки

До получения бухгалтерских документов использовать выражения `possible unexplained discrepancy`, `possible unauthorised retention` и `request for an evidence-based review`. Не утверждать как установленный факт, что деньги были похищены.

Первое обращение целесообразно подписать одному заявителю как члену семьи или предполагаемому правопреемнику. После сбора документов можно добавить доверенности и подписи остальных наследников.
